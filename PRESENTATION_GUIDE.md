### Slide 1: Title Slide & Team Roster
- **Speaker:** Member 1 (Lead)
- **Script:**
  > *"Respected evaluators and professors, good morning. We are presenting our B.Tech capstone project titled 'E-Learning Course Analytics System'. Our presentation is organized into 5 continuous sections where each team member will walk you through their specialized engineering subsystem."*

---

### PART 1: TEAM MEMBER 1 (Slides 2, 3, 4)
*Role: Project Leadership, Educational Motivation & Literature Review*

- **Slide 2 (Introduction & E-Learning Growth Paradigm):**
  > *"To begin Part 1: Higher education has transitioned toward online learning and asynchronous modules. This has generated huge volumes of behavioral data. However, administrators lack real-time visibility into whether students are genuinely progressing or quietly disengaging."*
- **Slide 3 (Problem Statement: The Silent Student Dropout Crisis):**
  > *"Global educational studies indicate that between 70% and 85% of online learners drop out before completion. Traditional LMS reporting is 'post-mortem'—instructors only discover dropouts at the end of the term. Our objective was to develop an automated system that detects student silence early."*
- **Slide 4 (Comparative Gap Analysis: Existing Systems vs. Ours):**
  > *"Comparing existing approaches against our system: conventional tools rely on static spreadsheets or expensive proprietary software with hardcoded numbers. Our proposed system introduces real-time relational analytics, zero hardcoded values, and automated inactivity detection. I now hand over to Member 2 for System Architecture and REST API Engineering."*

---

### PART 2: TEAM MEMBER 2 (Slides 5, 6, 7)
*Role: Backend Architecture, Flask REST APIs & Concurrency*

- **Slide 5 (System Architecture: 3-Tier Multi-Layer Design):**
  > *"Thank you Member 1. In Part 2, I present our system architecture. We implemented a 3-tier model: Tier 1 is our presentation layer running Bootstrap 5 and Chart.js. Tier 2 is our application layer powered by Python 3 and Flask REST APIs. Tier 3 is our data persistence layer in MySQL 8."*
- **Slide 6 (Flask REST API Routing & HTTP Status Contracts):**
  > *"We architected RESTful endpoints adhering strictly to HTTP conventions: 200 for retrieval, 201 for created records, 400 for bad input, 404 for missing IDs, and 409 Conflict for duplicate registrations. Every API response returns structured JSON with no exposed stack traces."*
- **Slide 7 (Backend Concurrency & Loopback Optimization):**
  > *"To ensure maximum speed, we enabled multi-threaded request processing in Flask so all dashboard cards load concurrently. We also standardized on direct IPv4 loopback (127.0.0.1), which eliminated Windows IPv6 DNS resolution latency and dropped connection time to 14ms. I now hand over to Member 3 for Database Engineering."*

---

### PART 3: TEAM MEMBER 3 (Slides 8, 9, 10)
*Role: Database Engineering, Relational Schema & Constraints*

- **Slide 8 (Database Architecture: MySQL 8.0 & Storage Engine):**
  > *"Thank you Member 2. In Part 3, I present our database architecture. We selected MySQL Server 8 with the InnoDB engine for strict ACID compliance. We placed B-tree indexes on activity dates and enrollment statuses to ensure sub-millisecond query performance."*
- **Slide 9 (Relational Schema: 4 Normalized Entities):**
  > *"Our relational schema is normalized into 4 entities: courses, learners, enrollments, and activities. Learners are identified by unique college roll numbers. Enrollments track progress status: Enrolled, In Progress, or Completed. Activities record timestamped student interactions."*
- **Slide 10 (Integrity Constraints & Safe Deletion Enforcement):**
  > *"We enforced referential integrity at the schema level: A composite UNIQUE key on (learner_id, course_id) prevents duplicate enrollments. Furthermore, ON DELETE RESTRICT prevents accidental deletion of courses that have students registered. I now hand over to Member 4 for Computational Analytics and Algorithms."*

---

### PART 4: TEAM MEMBER 4 (Slides 11, 12, 13)
*Role: Computational Analytics, Math Models & Inactivity Detection*

- **Slide 11 (Mathematical Formulations: Completion Analytics):**
  > *"Thank you Member 3. In Part 4, I present our analytical engine. Course completion rate is calculated as completed enrollments divided by total enrollments multiplied by 100, with safe division protection. Crucially, overall platform completion is calculated across all enrollments directly; we avoid the common mistake of averaging course percentages across unequal class sizes."*
- **Slide 12 (Algorithm: Inactive Enrollment Detection - 14-Day Rule):**
  > *"Our early-warning algorithm flags enrollments where status is not 'Completed' and either zero activities are logged or the latest activity is older than 14 days. By using GROUP BY on enrollment_id with SQL HAVING clauses, we guarantee zero duplicate rows even if a student has logged dozens of activities."*
- **Slide 13 (Weekly Activity Aggregation & Dual-Engine Architecture):**
  > *"We built a chronological resampling engine that groups student submissions by calendar week. We also engineered a dual-engine architecture: if Pandas is available, it uses vector math; if not, it automatically falls back to native Python datetime and SQL dictionaries without downtime. I now hand over to Member 5 for UI/UX, Security, and Testing."*

---

### PART 5: TEAM MEMBER 5 (Slides 14, 15, 16)
*Role: Frontend UI/UX, Security Hardening, QA & Benchmarks*

- **Slide 14 (User Interface: Dashboard & Visual Analytics):**
  > *"Thank you Member 4. In Part 5, I present our user interface. We designed a clean SaaS dashboard in Bootstrap 5. It features 6 real-time KPI cards, an interactive Chart.js bar chart displaying completion rates alongside student counts, and a chronological weekly activity trend line chart."*
- **Slide 15 (Security Hardening & Report Generation Pipeline):**
  > *"For security, 100% of SQL statements use parameterized queries to eliminate SQL injection. Database passwords are kept strictly in .env files. We also built an in-memory streaming pipeline to export 4 official academic reports to CSV with audit timestamps, as well as backend Matplotlib figure generation."*
- **Slide 16 (Verification Testing Suite & Latency Benchmarks):**
  > *"Our QA verification passed 14/14 automated unit and integration tests. Benchmarks demonstrated an instantaneous 94ms page render time and 14ms database loopback speed. All edge cases, including zero enrollments and duplicate inputs, were verified. I now hand back to Member 1 and the team for Future Scope and Conclusion."*
